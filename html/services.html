01 / Print

Digital printing

Choose the paper finish and ink that best suit the look and feel of your project.

Paper stocks

Glossy

Perfect for vibrant, high-resolution images, glossy paper enhances colors and details. Ideal for photographs, brochures, and high-quality prints.

Matte

With a smooth, non-reflective finish, matte paper suits text-heavy documents, professional presentations, and posters where readability matters.

Textured

Add a tactile touch to invitations, special announcements, and artistic projects with textured paper.

Hewlett Packard
